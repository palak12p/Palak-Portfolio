# Third-party software

The production bundle includes React, React DOM and Scheduler under their MIT licenses. Full notices are in `licenses/`, `public/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`, the built `dist/` folder, and an HTML comment in the standalone offline edition.

Vite, TypeScript, Playwright, axe-core and the type definitions are development tools. Their upstream license files are available in their npm distributions when dependencies are installed. They are not bundled into the visitor-facing application.

The portfolio's diagrams and icons were written for this project. Fonts are resolved from the visitor's system; font binaries are not redistributed. The resume is user-supplied material.
