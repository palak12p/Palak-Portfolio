# Authentic voice-use record

**Current status:** no Wispr Flow session or recording was verified during creation of this local folder. The site is not presented as evidence of voice use.

Use this structure after a real session. Keep recordings local until you decide what to share. Avoid showing passwords, private messages or unrelated account information.

## Record one entry per meaningful iteration

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Session date and duration | Actual date, time and approximate duration |
| Tool and account | Wispr Flow and the builder you actually used; keep private email details out of public material if desired |
| Goal | What you wanted to change and why |
| Your dictated instruction | An accurate transcript or short excerpt |
| Result | The specific screen, copy or behavior that changed |
| Review | What you accepted, corrected or rejected |
| Voice value | Your actual observation, including friction if there was any |
| Evidence file | Local recording filename or accessible recording URL |

## Useful evidence to capture

1. The start of a real Flow dictation in your builder.
2. A meaningful spoken instruction that expresses your intent.
3. The resulting iteration in the portfolio.
4. Your explanation of whether speaking changed your thinking, detail or speed.

Do not invent minutes saved, a speed multiplier, number of prompts, usage statistics or positive product feedback. If voice did not help with a task, say what happened. The strongest application will sound like your own account of building.
